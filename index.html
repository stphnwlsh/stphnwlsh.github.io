About

I'm a software engineering leader in Neenah, Wisconsin, by way of Australia. My favourite spot is where leading teams meets staying hands-on in the code: helping engineers grow while still caring about how the software is put together.

Outside of work I'm a husband and father first, a long-suffering Liverpool supporter, and a Movember regular. The moustache only visits in November, which everyone agrees is for the best.

Why I grow a mo: I've got more than one reason: my Dad, who won his fight against prostate cancer, my friend Bryan, who's in remission after his, and Derrick and Sean, who went far too soon. If you'd like to help change the face of men's health, every donation counts. Please give.
